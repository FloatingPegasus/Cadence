# Code of Conduct

Cadence is a collaborative project. Participants are expected to be welcoming,
respectful, and constructive, regardless of experience, identity, or
background.

Examples of helpful behavior include:

- explaining disagreements with evidence and curiosity;
- giving feedback about the work rather than the person; and
- respecting privacy and different perspectives.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, unwanted sexual
attention, and publishing someone else's private information are not welcome.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers
privately through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. The
maintainers will review reports and may remove comments, close discussions, or
restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe.
